Understanding Assisted Living

What Is Assisted Living?

Assisted Living Facilities (ALFs) provide housing, meals, and personal care services for seniors who need help with daily activities but don't require the intensive medical care of a nursing home. Residents maintain their independence while receiving support with things like bathing, dressing, medication management, and meals.

Assisted Living vs. Nursing Home

The key difference is the level of medical care. Assisted living focuses on personal care and daily living support in a home-like environment. Nursing homes provide 24/7 skilled nursing care for individuals with complex medical needs. Many families find assisted living offers the right balance of support and independence.

When Is It Time for Assisted Living?

Common signs include: difficulty managing medications, frequent falls, declining hygiene, social isolation, caregiver burnout, unsafe living conditions, or a dementia/Alzheimer's diagnosis. If you're noticing these signs, it may be time to explore assisted living options.

Assisted Living Tour Checklist

Use this checklist when visiting assisted living communities to make sure you ask the right questions:

Care & Staffing

  • What is the staff-to-resident ratio?
  • Are caregivers available 24/7?
  • How are care plans developed and updated?
  • What training do staff members receive?
  • How is medication managed?
  • Is there a nurse on staff or on-call?

Daily Life

  • What meals are provided? Can I see a sample menu?
  • What activities and programs are available?
  • Can residents personalize their rooms?
  • Are there outings and transportation services?
  • What are the visiting hours for family?
  • How are residents' preferences accommodated?

Costs & Policies

  • What is the monthly cost? What's included?
  • Are there additional fees for specific services?
  • What payment methods are accepted?
  • Is there a move-in deposit? Is it refundable?
  • What is the discharge/move-out policy?
  • Does the community accept long-term care insurance?

Safety & Licensing

  • Is the facility licensed by the state of Florida?
  • What is the latest inspection report?
  • What security measures are in place?
  • What is the emergency/disaster plan?
  • Are there call systems in resident rooms?
  • How are incidents reported to families?
