About Suncoast Senior Living

Committed to Compassionate Senior Care in Sarasota

Our Mission

At Suncoast Senior Living, our mission is simple: provide a safe, loving, and enriching environment where seniors can thrive. We believe every person deserves dignified care that honors their individuality, and we work every day to deliver on that promise.

Who We Are

Suncoast Senior Living is a family of assisted living communities located throughout Sarasota, Florida. With three distinct communities — Suncoast East, Suncoast Club at Prestancia, and Suncoast House — we offer families a range of options to find the perfect fit for their loved ones.

We're not a national chain or a corporate franchise. We're locally owned and operated, deeply rooted in the Sarasota community, and personally invested in the well-being of every resident who calls our communities home.

Our Approach to Care

We believe in person-centered care — meeting each resident where they are and tailoring our services to their evolving needs. Our approach includes:

  • Individualized Care Plans: Developed with families and medical professionals, reviewed and updated regularly.
  • Trained, Compassionate Staff: Our caregivers are carefully selected for their skill, empathy, and dedication.
  • Engaging Lifestyle: From social activities to chef-prepared meals, we create an environment where residents look forward to each day.
  • Family Partnership: We keep families informed and involved because we believe the best care is collaborative.
