About This Event

Separating hospice myths from realities

Hospice is one of the most misunderstood parts of senior care. Join us at Suncoast East for an honest, approachable conversation that clears up the myths and explains what hospice really offers — and when it can help.

Our featured speaker is Karen Novak, RN, MS, CDP — Professional Relations Liaison with Empath Health — who will share real-world insight and answer your questions.
