About This Event

Your Medicare questions, answered

Medicare can feel overwhelming — the plans, the options, the deadlines. Join us at Suncoast East for a relaxed, no-pressure session where you can ask anything and get clear, honest answers.

Janet Lambert of Lambert Insurance — a licensed insurance agent specializing in Medicare health plans — will be on hand to walk you through your choices and help you feel confident about your coverage. Whether you're new to Medicare or reviewing your current plan, all are welcome.
