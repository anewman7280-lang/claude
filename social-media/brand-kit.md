# Suncoast Senior Living — Social Media Brand Kit

The single source of truth for the monthly flyer program. Every flyer and
caption should pull its facts, colors, voice, and hashtags from here.

---

## 1. Brand voice

- **Warm, reassuring, and family-first.** We speak to adult children (45–65)
  making a hard, emotional decision for a parent, and to seniors themselves.
- **Trustworthy, never salesy.** Lead with care, dignity, and peace of mind.
  The tour/consult is always *free* and *no-obligation*.
- **Local and human.** We are Sarasota's neighbors, not a national chain.
- Avoid clinical jargon. Say "your loved one," "home," "peace of mind."
- Always accessible: describe images in alt text; keep text on flyers large.

## 8. Compliance / do & don't

- ✅ "Licensed & inspected," "free consultation," resident dignity.
- ❌ No medical claims or guarantees of outcomes.
- ❌ Never post a real resident photo without written consent — use the
  photo-slot placeholders until you have a consented image.
